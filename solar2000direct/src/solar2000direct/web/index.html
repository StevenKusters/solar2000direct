Meter cross-check

The inverter's CT-clamp meter against the utility's fiscal meter. They measure the same connection and will not agree exactly; a percent or two is normal, a persistent gap of more than that means a clamp is miscalibrated or reversed. Where the utility meter is readable, it is the one the money and the grid figures use, because it is the one that is billed.
